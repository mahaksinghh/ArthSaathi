Try a ₹2,450 sale

Meera Stores and both UPI IDs are fictional. The demo opens separately from your workspace.

  1. Open Check a QR for this sale. The pending sale supplies the expected amount.
  2. Choose Scan sample for this sale. The amount matches ₹2,450, but the QR encodes collection@demo instead of the expected meera@demo.
  3. Choose Save QR review to sale. The mismatch stays with the sale, which remains pending.
  4. Simulate checking the incoming credit in the bank app, then choose Record demo confirmation. The ledger records one receipt.

Why check the bank independently? A QR describes a destination or payment instruction. It cannot show whether money reached the merchant.

How ArthSaathi compares

Google Search, Lens and Safe Browsing

Search surfaces relevant information. Lens scans QR codes and can assess suspicious messages using AI and web sources. Safe Browsing checks URLs against Google's threat lists. Search help · QR scanning · Lens scam detection · Safe Browsing

Google Pay and PhonePe

These products support UPI payments and merchant services. Google Pay for Business offers fraud safeguards. PhonePe for Business tracks transactions and provides payment confirmations. Google Pay for Business · PhonePe merchant tools

NPCI MyUPI

MyUPI accepts a QR or UPI ID for payee details and provides UPI activity, complaint support and guidance. NPCI product overview

ArthSaathi's focus

Start with the merchant's own pending sale. Compare its expected details with the QR, keep the reasons beside the sale, and record the credit check in the ledger. The original QR image stays in the browser.

What runs in the live app

QR and message analysis

Canvas reads the image pixels and a Web Worker runs the bundled jsQR library. Plain JavaScript checks payment fields, compares the encoded UPI ID and amount with expected values, and explains message and link risk signals. These checks use no external scoring API.

Workspace and voice

Browser localStorage stores the regular workspace and saved review summaries. Web Speech handles dictation and read-aloud where supported. The browser's speech service may process audio.

Deployment and assistant

Vercel serves the static demo. Built-in answers run in the browser. An optional Node server exposes /api/status, /api/guide and /api/assistant. With a configured model, server credentials and user consent, both POST endpoints can call the OpenAI Responses API. Only the assistant endpoint enables the web search tool. That integration is disabled on the static demo.

Payment boundary

The demo uses no bank, UPI network, Google Safe Browsing, or payment gateway API. ArthSaathi cannot confirm account ownership, verify settlement, or move money. The user records their own independent credit check.
